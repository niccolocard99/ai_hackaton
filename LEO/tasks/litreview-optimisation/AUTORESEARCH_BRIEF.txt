--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. WHAT THE TASK IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Design trastuzumab variants that beat it on THREE measured properties:
  (1) binding affinity        - measured by SPR
  (2) epitope preservation    - competitive binding against trastuzumab
  (3) thermostability         - directly measured (Tm)
Only ~4 designs are guaranteed to be lab-tested, so RANK ORDER IS THE OUTPUT.
Precision beats volume. Ten designs improving one property each lose to two
that improve all three.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HARD CONSTRAINTS (verified against apheris/challenge/*.md, not just summaries)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Shared, every design:
  - numbering confidence >= 0.1; format VHH, scFv or Fab
  - total length <= 250 aa
  - NO UNPAIRED CYSTEINES
Optimisation track extra checks:
  - CDRH3 identity to trastuzumab < 70%
  - MORE THAN 2 point mutations from ANY provided data point (use 3 as minimum)
  - CDRH3 must differ from all 525,063 CDRH3s in the HER2 novelty set

Verified notes:
  - Deck says "<=70%", task description and flowchart say "<70%". MOOT for an
    11-residue CDRH3: k substitutions give (11-k)/11 identity, so k=3 -> 72.7%
    (fails) and k=4 -> 63.6% (passes). Nothing lands on 70%. MINIMUM IS 4.
  - Mutation count: 2 of 3 sources say "more than 2". Use 3 as the floor.
  - Track is assigned on identity to NEAREST KNOWN ANTIBODY, not to trastuzumab.
    Keeping trastuzumab's framework/light chain/5 CDRs means ~95% identity to a
    known antibody, so designs route to OPTIMISATION. Correct and intended.
  - A design is NEVER reclassified between tracks. Missing its track's criteria
    means rejection.

FORMAT ARITHMETIC (inference, verify):
  VHH  ~120-130 aa  -> comfortable under the 250 aa cap
  scFv ~245-255 aa  -> RIGHT AT the cap, tight
  Fab  ~435-445 aa  -> CANNOT FIT. Do not propose a Fab.

*** THREE CORRECTIONS. THE 1N8Z-ONLY ANALYSIS WAS TOO GENEROUS. ***

CORRECTION 1 - 1N8Z IS BLIND TO HER2 581-590.
That unresolved loop sits directly under CDRH3's C-terminal half. Verified two
ways: the chain C sequence has a 10-residue gap starting at 581, AND the
organisers' own file says so -
  apheris/rfd4/Data/12-her2/her2_cdrh3.yaml:
  "HER2 is split at its unresolved loop (581-590) into chains C (489-580) and
   D (591-607), because input validation rejects a numbering gap inside one
   chain."
So the 4.0-5.5 A cutoff sweep tested the wrong axis - every run used a
structure missing part of the epitope. 8Q6J DOES resolve that loop, and there
G100 contacts VAL582 and D102 contacts VAL582 3.42 / PRO579 3.43 / ASP570 3.86.

CORRECTION 2 - "CONTACT-FREE" IS NOT "FREE". MEASURED GEOMETRY (1N8Z):
  G100  CANNOT CARRY A SIDE CHAIN. A modelled CB lands 1.73 A from R98.NH2.
        Any side chain must displace R98, which anchors the D108 salt bridge.
        UNUSABLE.
  G101  phi = +107 deg (the epsilon region) - GLYCINE-SPECIFIC. A side chain
        there points toward HER2 I591 but K593 stays 8.2-8.4 A away at every
        side-chain length, so a charge buys no salt bridge. High risk, low gain.
  D102  THE FREEST SIDE CHAIN IN THE LOOP (2.0 intramolecular neighbours per
        atom). Its HER2 "contact" is made by the BACKBONE CARBONYL O - no
        side-chain substitution can remove it. Excluding D102 while including
        G100/G101 was backwards.
  F104  F104->Y IS STERICALLY DEAD: the ring's para axis points AWAY from K593
        and a modelled Tyr OH clashes at 2.90 A with VL L46 and 2.93 A with
        VL Y55. Do not do it.
  A106  3.08 A clearance in the best of 2000 sampled directions => SER ONLY.
  M107  MOST LOAD-BEARING IN THE LOOP (18 neighbours per side-chain atom):
        W110, W99, W47, H35, V37 + VL Y36, Q89, P96, F98. This is the
        FR2<->CDR3 class whose loss caused the triple failure in 36153698.
        0.0 A^2 solvent-accessible - a buried core residue.
  D108  BIDENTATE SALT BRIDGE TO R98: OD2-NH2 2.69 A, OD1-NE 3.00 A. Near-
        structural; substitute isoelectronically (D->E) or not at all.
  Y109  Its OH is UNSATISFIED (nearest polar partner 4.12 A) => Y109F is the
        cheapest substitution available.
=> THE REAL RISK AXIS IS THE PER-POSITION INTRAMOLECULAR CONTACT BUDGET, NOT
   THE HER2 CONTACT COUNT. All candidate designs preserve 100% of HER2
   contacts, so that metric has ZERO resolving power between them. The
   literature agrees this is the real failure mode: 34392800 (affinity varies
   through H3 secondary-structure change, NOT contact loss), 36153698,
   28928732 (gains were "generally NOT due to direct interactions involving
   the mutated residues but indirect effects").

CORRECTION 3 - W99 IS NOT THE CONSERVED BETA-BULGE TRYPTOPHAN.
An earlier version of this file said it was (citing 38916527). Wrong: that
conserved Trp is Kabat W103 = W110 here, in FR4, OUTSIDE CDRH3 and untouchable
regardless. W99 is Kabat H95, the ceiling/class-switch residue of 20961619 -
and that paper's ceiling effect VANISHED at 11 residues, which is our length,
so that rationale is weaker than it looked. W99 stays untouchable anyway
because it is a measured contact in 4/5 structures with ~150 intramolecular
neighbours. Conclusion unchanged, stated reason corrected.

*** THE IDENTITY-BOUNDARY TRAP - RESOLVE THIS BEFORE FREEZING ANY LIST ***
The organisers' yaml designates the designable window B99-108 and pins
B[1-98, 109-120], i.e. it treats Y109 AS FRAMEWORK and CDRH3 as a 10-mer.
scripts/_track_common.py computes:
    cdrh3_identity(a,b) = 1 - levenshtein(a,b) / max(len(a), len(b))
On the 10-mer reading, 3 substitutions give EXACTLY 70.0%, which is NOT
strictly under 70% and therefore FAILS. On the 11-mer reading 4 subs give
63.6% and 5 give 54.5%.
=> Within 99-108 the usable positions are {101, 102, 106, 107, 108} (100 is
   sterically locked). Excluding BOTH G101 and A106 leaves only {102, 107,
   108} = 3 subs = 70.0% = FAIL. So EVERY BOUNDARY-ROBUST DESIGN MUST SPEND A
   SUBSTITUTION AT EITHER G101 (Ramachandran-penalised) OR A106 (forced to
   Ser, uncertain sign). There is no way around this.
=> RUN scripts/check_optimisation.py AND READ THE cdrh3 COLUMN abguard
   REPORTS. One command. It settles the denominator.

*** D102 [Kabat D98] IS THE BEST POSITION TO MUTATE, NOT ONE TO AVOID ***
An earlier version of this brief said to avoid D102 because it contacts at a
5.5 A cutoff. THAT WAS WRONG. The measured evidence says the opposite:
  - Ala-scan effect only 1.5-2x: it is NOT a hotspot.
  - US-8840896-B2: D98 was "the most poorly conserved residue", mutated in 98%
    of selected phage clones, most frequent substitution Trp (23%).
  - D98W gives a 3-FOLD IMPROVEMENT in affinity over WT hu4D5-8 Fab by SPR.
  - D98F also gains affinity, removing repulsion with HER2 D586/E598 (31891584).
  - It is at "the tip of variable loop 3" - the apex, exactly where the loop
    literature says to concentrate change.
  CAVEAT: D98W is PUBLISHED, so a design must stay >=3 mutations from that
  single mutant. D102W combined with 3+ other substitutions is fine; a design
  whose only change is D102W is invalid.

The four critical paratope residues overall by Ala scan (50-5000x): heavy R50,
W95, Y100a; light H91. Light H91->Ala alone costs 200-fold and is only 22%
solvent-exposed - another reason to keep the light chain fixed.

G103 [G99] is untouchable for TWO independent reasons: the 2.42 A contact with
no side-chain room, AND "V_H99 was a highly conserved residue in variable loop
3 ... position i+2 of a type II beta-turn which is most commonly a Gly ...
likely to be a structure-stabilizing residue" (US-8840896-B2). One dissent:
31891584 proposed G99D/R as a deliberate loop size increase to modulate
electrostatics with HER2 D570 - high-risk/high-reward, not a default.

HER2 EPITOPE (measured, 19 residues, span 557-605 = 49 aa end to end, so
strongly discontinuous - this CONFIRMS by measurement a claim that previously
rested on one conference abstract):
  PRO557 GLU558 ASP560 GLN561 LYS569 ASP570 PRO571 PRO572 PHE573 PRO579
  ILE591 LYS593 ASP596 GLU598 ALA600 GLN602 PRO603 CYS604 PRO605
  - heavy chain engages 557-593; light chain engages 560-605
  - ASP560, PRO571, PRO572 are contacted by BOTH chains (shared hot spots)
  - epitope is PROLINE-RICH and CHARGE-DENSE -> preserve net charge and polar
    character of any rewritten CDRH3 face

*** KNOWN BAD DATA - DO NOT USE ***
A conference abstract (doi 10.1096/fasebj.31.1_supplement.775.23) reports HER2
contact loops at 991-995, 1004-1007, 1027-1037 and claims to analyse 1N8Z.
These are INCOMPATIBLE: 1N8Z's HER2 chain is numbered 1-607 and residues 991+
are intracellular in full-length HER2. Use the measured 557-605 set instead.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE RISK THAT THE ABOVE STRATEGY CREATES - READ BEFORE DESIGNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provided data includes 524,346 CDRH3 variants WITH UP TO 10 MUTATED POSITIONS.
The rule requires >2 mutations from ANY provided data point = Hamming distance
>= 3 from all 524,346.

Restricting changes to 6 contact-free positions SHRINKS the design space, and
the binding-compatible corner of a small space is exactly where a
binder-selected FACS library is dense. A candidate 1-2 mutations from any
library variant is REJECTED, not reclassified.

Mitigations (apply all four):
  1. OVER-SHOOT: aim for 5 substitutions not 4, and Hamming >= 4 not 3. Both
     thresholds are cliff edges and an extra contact-free substitution is cheap.
  2. GENERATE A SURPLUS - some candidates will die at novelty validation.
  3. The novelty check CANNOT RUN on a machine without
     apheris/hackathon-data/her2-trastuzumab/. A locally clean candidate means
     nothing until scripts/check_optimisation.py has run on the VM.
  4. Weak argument in our favour (needs checking against the tables): a FACS
     screen selected for BINDERS, so it had less reason to systematically
     explore contact-free positions.

4. DESIGN RULES FROM THE LITERATURE (pmid-cited; details in findings/01-04)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCIENTIFIC LICENSE FOR HEAVY REWRITING
  - An anti-HER2 VHH with NO light chain and none of trastuzumab's CDRs binds
    trastuzumab's OWN epitope at sub-nM affinity (37632647).
  - bH1/bH1-44, radically re-engineered Herceptin paratopes, KEEP Herceptin-level
    HER2 affinity; binding switches enthalpy-driven -> entropy-driven (21526167).
  - Best published affinity gains come from 4-6 SIMULTANEOUS CDR mutations, and
    the gains are mostly INDIRECT effects enhancing contacts of UNMUTATED
    residues (28928732).
  - A 20-aa CDRH3 split into 4 sub-libraries, randomised separately and
    recombined, gave 1230-fold on HER2 (KD 13 pM) (8918938).

WHERE TO SPEND SUBSTITUTIONS
  - Apex and C-terminal half are contact-enriched (11335716, 25484051).
  - Treat the H3 BASE as structural. Residue H95-equivalent sets a ~100-fold
    "ceiling affinity" and acts as a class switch; mutations elsewhere have
    RECIPROCAL effects between classes (20961619).
  - Keep LOOP LENGTH CONSTANT at 11. Length change rewires the epistasis (the
    H95 ceiling effect held at 9 residues and vanished at 11) (20961619), and
    structure prediction degrades: RosettaAntibody median H3 RMSD 1.6 A (4-6 aa),
    2.4 A (10-11), 3.1 A (12-14), 6.0 A (17-22) (19062174).
  - Pick positions by contact/permissiveness, NOT by somatic-hypermutation
    hotspot propensity (15777944).
  - Changes OUTSIDE H3 are free against the identity budget (L3, H2, framework).
  - Preserve the C-terminal kink (25579815, 24519881) and the base Trp (38916527).

RESIDUE CHEMISTRY
  - Preserve net charge / polar character (binding is electrostatics-dominant,
    23955267; epitope is charge-dense per section 2).
  - DO NOT add aromatics expecting gains: two interface tryptophans made a
    design 6-fold WORSE (42909185).
  - Arginine only in hydrophobic microenvironments, and affinity must not depend
    on it (28778924); pair an introduced Arg with an Asp (26132144).
  - Prefer natural-diversity substitutions - residues frequent at that position
    in functional antibodies (28928732, 24116173).

HARD PRE-SYNTHESIS FILTERS
  - NO new N-glycosylation sequon (N-X-S/T, X != P) anywhere in or SPANNING the
    loop junctions. Three otherwise-good variants were lost to a CDR glycan that
    phage/bacterial screening could not see (24492299). The loop sits in context
    ...YYCSR-[WGGDGFYAMDY]-WGQGT... so check across both junctions.
  - No free cysteine (also a track rule). Never delete the canonical intradomain
    disulfides (1736986). An odd Cys does its damage IN A CDR (24598312).
  - Avoid engineered H3 disulfide staples - they form but over-constrain (11335716).

THERMOSTABILITY
  - FREE WIN, MEASURED ON TRASTUZUMAB scFv: VL-R66G gives Tm +5 C with
    antigen-binding affinity UNCHANGED (37396255). Designed by rigidifying a
    flexible region OUTSIDE the CDRs and OUTSIDE the VH-VL interface, so it is
    orthogonal to a CDRH3 rewrite. Caveat: single paper, E. coli-expressed scFv,
    effect measured on WT CDRs; additivity with a rewritten H3 is untested.
  - Trastuzumab's own Fv (clone 4D5) is used in the literature as the GOLD-
    STANDARD STABLE scFv framework (10582696, 10393549). So Tm gains must come
    from format (VHH removes the VH-VL interface failure mode), an engineered
    disulfide, or not wrecking the loops - NOT from a better framework.
  - If CDR grafting costs stability, buy it back at FR2 and the framework
    residues packing against CDR3 (36153698) - that paper is our exact failure
    mode in a VHH.
  - A pre-stabilised CDR-H3 ENABLES affinity optimisation; antibodies lacking H3
    stabilisation showed NO affinity improvement from the same process (41613601).
  - 4 mutations in CDR-L3 alone gave >18-fold affinity AND improved thermal
    stability (23995618). CDR-L3 is not in our epitope-critical set -> cheap
    place to recover both properties.
  - VH44-VL100 is the standard interdomain disulfide: GdnHCl50 2.4 -> 4.2 M with
    affinity unaffected (21339972).

TOOLING - what to use and what to avoid
  - Thermostability ranking: antibody-specific pLM / Rosetta-feature CNN
    (36683173) for scFv - out-of-distribution rho 0.52/0.40 vs 0.15 for general
    pLMs. TEMPRO (39154093) only for VHH; its RMSE 5.66 C is LARGER than the
    +5 C effect of a real stabilising mutation, so use it to flag outliers, never
    to rank two plausible designs.
  - Aggregation/solubility: Aggrescan3D or A4D, plus DeepSP (38827232,
    antibody-specific, r 0.76-0.96 vs MD). Protein-Sol is the only tool trained
    on CELL-FREE solubility data (28575391).
  - DO NOT rely on: SoluProt (AUC 0.62, near coin-flip, 33416864); raw Rosetta
    Cartesian_ddG ("high false discovery rate", 40643334); TemStaPro or ESMStabP
    (no antibody validation); "ProteinMPNN with soluble weights" (NO validation
    record in 505 records examined - it appears only inside one abstract).
  - Inverse folding: use an ANTIBODY-specific model. On nanobody CDR3, AntiBMPNN
    reached 1.7 nM while AntiFold and vanilla ProteinMPNN gave NO detectable
    activity (40576473). General-protein models "struggle with antibody-specific
    nuances" (40465588).
  - ProteinMPNN IS strong for FRAMEWORK-ONLY redesign with CDRs frozen: it
    converted 19/26 antibody sequences into functional scFv intrabodies, 18 of
    which had failed the standard approach (39975170).
  - Honest ceiling: on 80 blinded clinical antibodies, the best of 113 teams got
    Spearman rho 0.392 for thermostability, 0.310 for titer (41724677). Use
    predictors to DROP OUTLIERS and BREAK TIES, not to fine-rank.
  - ELISA signal is NOT affinity - two failed designs gave HIGHER ELISA signal
    while being 2-6x worse by Biacore (42909185).

CELL-FREE EXPRESSION (Adaptyv expresses cell-free; a design that does not
express cannot be measured at all)
  - Base rate: of 50 de novo CDR-designed scFvs, ROUGHLY HALF expressed
    detectably and 4 bound (28860479). With 4 slots, expect 1-2 silent failures.
  - Dominant failure mode is aggregation, not transcription - scFvs partition to
    the insoluble fraction and need refolding (42697344).
  - DsbC concentration was CRITICAL for soluble antibody fragments (29462206).
  - ACTION ITEM: confirm with Adaptyv whether their lysate is BACTERIAL or
    EUKARYOTIC. A signal peptide is MANDATORY in CHO-lysate CFPS (28931913) and
    harmful otherwise.
  - A single-chain format eliminates the HC:LC stoichiometry failure mode (36978695).

5. BASELINE NUMBERS - what we have and what is still missing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HAVE:
  - Trastuzumab-HER2 parent KD 0.058 nM, with a computationally designed TRIPLE
    mutant 32-fold better by SPR (28750054, ADAPT platform).
  - Herceptin whole IgG thermal stability ~75 C by far-UV CD (31071101).
  - 193 single-mutant trastuzumab affinity values exist, used to train an
    epistasis-aware model; affinity variation traced to mutation-induced
    SECONDARY-STRUCTURE CHANGE IN CDR-H3 (34392800).
  - DSF reads on average 3 C LOWER than DSC (25385011) - do not mix methods.

  *** THE FAB Tm BASELINE - FOUND (pmid 21526167, DSC) ***
  - HERCEPTIN FAB T_M = 83 C. Conditions: DSC, 10 mM NaOAc pH 5 + 150 mM NaCl,
    0.5 mg/mL, 1 C/min. THIS IS THE NUMBER TO BEAT.
    Caveats: it is a FAB not an scFv, and pH 5 not physiological. DSF reads ~3 C
    LOWER than DSC (25385011), so compare like with like.
  - Same paper, same method, re-engineered Herceptin paratopes: bH1 77 C,
    bH1-81 76 C, bH1-44 74 C. ALL THREE LOST 6-9 C versus the parent Fab.
    That is the first real quantification of what rewriting this paratope costs
    in stability, and it is substantial. Budget for it.

  *** FULL THERMODYNAMICS - FOUND (pmid 21526167, ITC + SPR) ***
    K_D   0.5 nM           SPR, BIAcore 3000, 30 C, 1:1 Langmuir
    k_on  7e5 M-1 s-1      SPR, 30 C
    k_off 4e-4 s-1         SPR, 30 C
    dH    -13.5 kcal/mol   ITC, VP-ITC, PBS pH 7.4, 30 C
    -TdS  +0.9 kcal/mol    from dG(SPR) and dH(ITC)
    dCp   -370 cal/mol.K   ITC, dH vs T, 20-37 C
    => BINDING IS ENTHALPY-DRIVEN; the entropy term is UNFAVOURABLE.
       bH1's rewritten paratope switched to ENTROPY-driven binding, so a heavily
       rewritten CDRH3 may bind by a different thermodynamic route. Not
       necessarily worse, but different - do not assume the parent's signature.

  *** INTERFACE SIZE - FOUND ***
    1350 A^2 total BSA (crystal, 37569408); ~1675 A^2 from MD models (21600050)
    910 A^2 buried APOLAR area; interface is ~60% APOLAR (21526167)
    Binding is essentially RIGID-BODY (dCp-derived vs observed apolar area agree
    to 3%) (21526167)

  *** K_D DISCREPANCY TO RESOLVE BEFORE CLAIMING A BASELINE ***
    0.058 nM (28750054, SPR) vs 0.5 nM (21526167, SPR 30 C) - about 10x apart,
    both SPR. Likely IgG vs Fab and/or temperature/format. DECIDE WHICH BASELINE
    WE CLAIM AGAINST AND STATE THE METHOD. For reference, 28750054's best triple
    mutant reached K_D 1.9 pM, ~30x better than parent, via ~6x k_on and ~5x k_off.

STILL MISSING:
  - NO QUANTIFIED dTm COST per CDRH3 substitution. We know the bH1 series lost
    6-9 C for a whole-paratope rewrite, but nothing gives per-mutation cost.
  - No per-CDR BSA breakdown and no per-residue dG for the paratope. The Ala scan
    gives BINNED FOLD-CHANGES, not dG values.
  - No crystallographic H-bond / salt-bridge table or distance list. The only
    explicit pairings (23955267) are MD on a TRUNCATED 18-MER PEPTIDE, not the
    crystal contact list.
  - No kink/bulge call or canonical-class assignment for trastuzumab's CDRH3.
    Every corpus paper on H3 canonical clusters was checked individually and NONE
    mentions trastuzumab, Herceptin, 4D5 or 1N8Z.
  - pmid 12610629 (the 2.5 A primary structure) is NOT in the corpus, and 1N8Z's
    resolution is never stated in any record we hold.
  - pmid 42968171 / PDB 7PKL is absent from the corpus entirely.
  - No published trastuzumab variant with >=4 CDRH3 substitutions and a measured
    KD. The <70%-identity regime is literally unmapped for trastuzumab.
