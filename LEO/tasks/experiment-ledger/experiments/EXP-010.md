## Hypothesis, stated before the analysis

That the 20 designs in `design-loop/out/FINAL.csv` are consistent with TH-020's position map, and that
scoring them with the frozen stage-1 models adds information the design loop did not already have.

## What actually happened

### The position strategy is executed correctly — this is the thing to keep

Substitutions across the 20 designs, against TH-020's measured per-position effects:

| position | WT | mutated in | TH-020 effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| W99 | W | **0 / 20** | hurts (q<0.001) |
| G100 | G | 14 / 20 | **helps** (q=0.062) |
| G101 | G | **20 / 20** | **helps** (q=0.077) |
| D102 | D | 16 / 20 | inert |
| G103 | G | 2 / 20 | hurts (q=0.090) |
| F104 | F | **0 / 20** | hurts (q=0.028) |
| Y105 | Y | **0 / 20** | hurts (q<0.001) |
| A106 | A | 20 / 20 | inert |
| M107 | M | **0 / 20** | hurts (q=0.040) |
| D108 | D | 20 / 20 | inert |

34 substitutions land on the two helpful positions, 56 on the three inert ones, and **only 2 on a harmful
one** (G103, in ranks 13 and 14). **18 of 20 designs touch no harmful position at all.** Given the rule
forces ≥4 substitutions, spending them on inert positions is the right play, and that is what happened.

### The two binding surrogates agree, which is real corroboration

Spearman(design-loop `kd_mean`, frozen-stage-1 `pred_log10_kd`) = **+0.941**, p < 1e-9. Our loop's bootstrap
ranker and the plain out-of-fold ranker order these windows the same way. The *final ranks* agree at only
+0.030 (p=0.90), which is correct and expected: the loop ranks three properties, stage 1 sees one. **Stage 1
must not be used to re-rank this list.**

### Three slots are spent on a change nothing can score

For the three windows that appear both with and without `D28K`, `kd_mean`, `dtm_vs_wt` and `epi_risk` are
*identical*, yet the pairs land at ranks 2/10, 5/17 and 19/4:

| window | kd_mean | dtm_vs_wt | epi_risk | ranks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WKSQGFYEMI | −1.153 both | 0.05 both | 1.375 both | 19 and 4 |
| WTVFGFYEME | −0.493 both | 1.55 both | 1.375 both | 5 and 17 |
| WWVEGFYEMI | −1.091 both | −0.32 both | 1.375 both | 2 and 10 |

`D28K` is a VL substitution; the ranker was fitted on trastuzumab's light chain and the Tm model is
heavy-only, so no scorer in the loop can see it. Those pairs are separated only by tie-breaking.

`fwTm` is the opposite and is a genuine lever: its pairs differ by **+2.4 to +2.6 °C** of predicted Tm,
matching `NICCOLO/thermo/` that framework substitutions are the only thermostability handle.

### Other measurements

- **20 rows, 14 distinct CDRH3 loops.** Six loops appear twice under different add-ons.
- **7 of 20 binding predictions are below log10 KD −1.02**, i.e. tighter than anything measured in the legal
  band — extrapolation. The loop's own `kd_mean` flags 6.
- **Interpolation check passes:** every design is 3–4 substitutions from its nearest variant in the ranker's
  179-variant training set, and **none is at ≥5**, where measured signal is Spearman −0.057. Unusual and
  worth knowing: these scores sit inside the range where the ranker has demonstrated power.

## What it implies

- **TH-020 corroborated in practice.** Its position map was followed, independently of whether the loop read
  it, and the result is 18 of 20 designs avoiding every costly position.
- **TH-019 unchanged.** Nothing here beats trastuzumab; 7 of 20 only appear to because they extrapolate.
- **IDEA-008's recipe is satisfied** by the list as built (G100/G101 heavy, inert fillers, costly positions
  spared), so that idea can be closed by this list rather than needing a new design run.

## Honest limits

- Stage 1 reads the 10-residue window only. It is blind to `D28K` and to the 12 `fwTm` framework
  substitutions, so its agreement with `kd_mean` is agreement about *loops*, not about molecules.
- The +0.941 correlation is partly structural: the loop's ranker is built on the same two linear models
  (`design-loop/models.py` imports `prefilter.py`). It is a consistency check, not an independent one.
