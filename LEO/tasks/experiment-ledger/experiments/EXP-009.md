## Hypothesis, stated before the analysis

That EXP-008's two conclusions survive the full 179-variant table: the legal-band
hit rate against trastuzumab stays near 1 in 21, and the A106/M107/D102
convergence across the tightest legal variants holds as a substitution pattern
worth designing toward.

## What actually happened

Both failed. EXP-008 saw 21 legal variants; the full table has **131**.

### 1. The hit rate is six times rarer

| Baseline | Legal variants beating it |
|---|---|
| 0.152 nM (the single row EXP-008 used) | **1 of 131 (0.8%)** |
| 0.135 nM (median of trastuzumab's 8 replicate rows) | 1 of 131 (0.8%) |
| 0.029 nM (best replicate) | **0 of 131** |

EXP-008 inferred 4.8%. The winner is the same variant, `WGGSGPYLFD` (0.095 nM).
Two additions: trastuzumab's 8 replicates span **0.029–0.178 nM**, a 6x range, so
the baseline has to be named; and **all 131 are in the provided HER2 data**, so
every one of them fails the novelty filter, not just the winner.

### 2. The convergence was a base-rate artefact

| Position | Mutated in all 131 legal | Mutated in the 8 tightest |
|---|---|---|
| M107 | **92%** | 75% |
| D102 | 86% | 100% |
| A106 | 66% | 75% |

M107 is mutated *less* often among the tightest than at baseline. "Mutated in 8
of 8 winners" carries no information when it is mutated in 92% of everything.

### 3. Two of the three positions are measurably harmful

## Crossing this with EXP-002's contact table

| Position | Contacts HER2 | Affinity effect of mutating |
|---|---|---|
| **G100** | 3/8 | **helps** |
| **G101** | 2/8 | **helps** |
| A106 | **0/8** | inert |
| D108 | **0/8** | inert |
| D102 | 6/8 | inert |
| **M107** | **0/8** | **hurts** |
| F104 | 4/8 | hurts |
| W99 / G103 / Y105 | 5/8 / 7/8 / 7/8 | hurt |

**M107 never contacts HER2 in any of 8 structures and is still the costliest
position to mutate.** Non-contact predicts *tolerance*, not freedom. That is the
flaw in reasoning from contact geometry alone, and it is why both versions of
IDEA-008 spent a substitution there.

## Why we think it happened

EXP-008 conditioned on winners without checking the base rate among non-winners,
on a sample of 21 drawn from a set that was built for fold calibration — 30
binders spanning the KD range — not sampled. Any position mutated in ~90% of the
population will appear in ~90% of any small subset of it, winners included.

## What it implies

- **TH-019 revised, confidence held at `high`.** Its claim gets *stronger*: not
  rare but demonstrated, rather effectively absent — 0.8%, one variant, and that
  variant unsubmittable. Binding alone is a weaker S-tier basis than recorded.
- **TH-018 unaffected.** The ~4x cost of the legal band is arithmetic and holds.
- **TH-015 weakened where it is thin.** Its standout per-position residues rested
  on n=4–5. The position-level direction above rests on n=131 and disagrees at
  M107, which TH-015's table ranked as merely "costing affinity" rather than the
  single worst position to touch.
- **TH-009 split in two.** "Does not contact" conflates three things, which the
  data separates: *tolerated* (A106, D108 — never contact, affinity-inert),
  *beneficial* (G100, G101 — low contact, affinity helps) and *costly* (M107 —
  never contacts, affinity hurts). Contact frequency bounds epitope risk; it does
  not predict affinity.
- **TH-020 (new, `high`).** The per-position affinity map, and the rule that a
  "winners share X" claim is uninterpretable without X's base rate.

## IDEA-008, revised again

Drop M107 and F104. The recipe both datasets jointly support:

**G100 + G101 + A106 + D108** — 4 substitutions, 7/11 = 63.6% identity, gate
cleared. G100/G101 improve measured affinity and are the lowest-contact of the
contacting positions (3/8, 2/8), so epitope risk is low; A106/D108 never contact
and are affinity-inert, so they are free filler to reach four. It avoids every
position that either contacts heavily or costs affinity, and it does not depend
on Y109, which the SPR table cannot see.

## Honest limits

- **This is a landscape map, not a model.** All 131 variants are in the provided
  data and none is submittable. They say where substitutions are cheap, not that
  any particular design will work.
- **M107's effect size is uncertain**: n=11 conserved. The sign is consistent
  across two independent estimators, but the 129-vs-17 nM gap is not well
  determined.
- **G100/G101 sit at q=0.062 and 0.077**, above the usual 0.05. They are the best
  available bet, not a proven effect.
- **The position map adds no ranking power.** The pre-filter's ridge already
  encodes it: its implied per-position costs agree with the measured effects at
  Spearman +0.794 across the ten positions, and adding a costly-position counter
  changes its out-of-fold score by +0.002, 95% CI [-0.005, +0.011]. The map is for
  deciding *where to put substitutions when generating* a design; the ranker is
  for scoring the result. An earlier draft of the cross-check quoted this as a
  better predictor (+0.505 against +0.234 for mutation count) — that was an
  in-sample univariate correlation compared against an out-of-fold score, and it
  has been retracted.
- Binding only. Nothing here speaks to thermostability or epitope preservation,
  and epitope preservation is scored by competition, which no SPR number settles.
